# Steam

CabinetOS Handheld can hand the TV over to Steam's Big Picture for your PC games, and
take it back when Steam closes.

![Steam's Big Picture on the console](../media/steam-bigpicture.webp)

## Set it up

1. Press **Start** on Home (or the power button) for the Power menu, then
   **Switch to Steam**.
2. The first time, choose **Install**. Steam needs at least 100 GB: it gets its
   own space on the console's drive (a quarter of the drive to start with,
   between 100 and 500 GB). *Installing Steam* shows the progress; **B**
   cancels.
3. Sign in to Steam. Steam, its games and its settings are Steam's own, and it
   updates itself while it is open.

After that, **Switch to Steam** asks *Start Steam?* and goes straight in.

## Coming back

Close Steam from its own Power menu and the console comes back on Home. While
the console runs, nothing of Steam runs.

## Space and hiding

- Settings, Storage, **Steam** shows its space, with **Adjust storage** and
  **Remove Steam** (behind the PIN).
- Choose **Hide Steam** the first time to take it off the Power menu. Settings,
  System, **Steam** (*Hidden*) brings it back.
- With a PIN set, people other than the owner need it to use Steam.

Your Steam games are played in Steam, not from the console's Library.
