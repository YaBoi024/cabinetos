# Achievements

CabinetOS earns [RetroAchievements](https://retroachievements.org) in the
built-in emulators: every system from the Atari 2600 to the Dreamcast, PS1,
PSP, DS, GameCube, Wii and PS2. In arcade games, only where FinalBurn Neo
plays the game, since RetroAchievements' arcade sets are written for it. Not
in the separate emulators (Switch, PS3, Xbox, Xbox 360, Wii U).

## Sign in

Settings, Accounts, **RetroAchievements**. Type your RetroAchievements user
name, then your password. *Signed in as ...* means done. The console keeps a
sign-in token, not your password. Each person on the console signs in as
themselves.

Press the row again to sign out.

## While you play

- A small card in the top corner says *Achievement unlocked*, with its badge and
  name. It never pauses the game.
- Settings, Display and Sound, **Achievement sound** sets how loud it is (it
  appears once you are signed in).

## On a game's page

A **trophy** beside the heart, for games that have achievements. It is gold once
you have them all. Press Up then Right to reach it and **A** to see the list:
how many you have, and the points for each.

Achievements are earned in softcore mode: save states, rewind and the rest stay
available. A save state keeps where each achievement stood, as RetroArch's
states do, so one that counts something over time carries on counting from
the state, here or on another console.
