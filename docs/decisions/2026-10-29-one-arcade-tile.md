# One Arcade tile for every arcade folder

- **Decided:** The Library shows one tile, **Arcade**, holding the games of every RomM platform the console takes as arcade (`arcadematch::isArcadeSlug`), in RomM's title order with greyed games last, as every grid. In its grid the line beside the heading names the focused game's RomM folder after its name (*Metal Slug · NeoGeoMVS*). Everywhere else an arcade game's system is *Arcade*: Search, collections, Home, the game's page and Settings' Downloads. A missing BIOS names the folder it is uploaded to (*No Naomi BIOS on the server*). A game in two folders shows twice. There is no setting to turn it off and no filter; a RomM collection holds one board on its own. Each game keeps its own platform for its emulator, BIOS, discs, saves and states.
- **Date:** 2026-10-29
- **Why:** MMagTech: separate platforms are the right way to organise a RomM server, but at the TV a player picks a game, not a board, and a dozen arcade tiles crowd the Library for one kind of game. RomM lists several platforms in one request, in its title order across all of them and paged as one list, so the tile's grid works as any platform's and RomM is never changed. The line names what its heading does not, as for #579: under Arcade, the folder; under a heading that names no system, Arcade. A BIOS goes into one folder, so its message names that folder. A game in two folders is the admin's to tidy in RomM.
