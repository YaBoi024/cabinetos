# A guitar plays PS2 and PS3 games as a guitar

- **Decided:** A Guitar Hero or Rock Band guitar plays every PlayStation 2 and PlayStation 3 game as a guitar: PCSX2 is told the player's port holds its guitar, RPCS3 that the player holds a PS3 guitar, as the game starts. On PS3 a game whose name has "Rock Band" gets RPCS3's Rock Band guitar and every other game its Guitar Hero guitar. A controller is a guitar when it says so: an Xbox 360 guitar, bass or alternate guitar by the XInput subtype in its USB descriptors, or the word "guitar" in its USB product string or the kernel's name for its device; a guitar measured on a console whose whammy is elsewhere gets a row of its own. Its Home opens the pause menu in every game, and Controllers names it "Guitar". Every other system plays it as the pad it says it is, and no other pad changes. No setting. Bluetooth, cable and a guitar's own receiver alike. RedOctane's Xbox 360 X-plorer is covered from its documented layout and marked untested on real hardware until one is tried.
- **Date:** 2026-11-20
- **Why:** PS2 and PS3 together hold every Guitar Hero game in Redump's lists; Xbox 360 was dropped, and our Dolphin core has no Wii guitar. Each PS3 series looks for its own guitar, so one product for every game would leave half of them without one; the name is the one thing that tells the series apart. A list of brands would miss most guitars: SDL 3.4.18 calls a guitar on Linux a gamepad, and SDL's own list is per brand and holds none of the ids measured on CRKD's Les Paul, which shows Microsoft's Xbox 360 pad id on a cable and the Xbox One S pad's over Bluetooth. Only the guitar's own words say what it is. RetroBat alone does PS2 guitars, from a one-entry list and an admin switch; nobody does PS3 guitars. Players are told the slider's PC position and dial 8, CRKD's mode for community games, where whammy and tilt sit as on Microsoft's guitar. The kind is set at the start because RPCS3 reads its pads only then. A tilt is held on for 0.3 s after each reading that says tilted, since the Les Paul's cable flickers.
