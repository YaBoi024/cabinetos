# Xbox 360 draws at the Xenos sample positions, for every game

- **Decided:** Xenia Edge moves from `a7c39fa` to `4ad875a`, and the console passes `--xenos_sample_positions=true` and `--window_offset_in_edram=false` for every Xbox 360 game. That takes the black patches off characters in Gears of War, Gears of War 2 and the other Unreal Engine 3 games that tile their anti-aliasing. No settings file per game.
- **Date:** 2026-11-01 (approved by MMagTech, and judged by him on the TV in Gears of War, Gears of War 2 and Forza Horizon 2)
- **Why:** The pin moved for reason B of [when-an-emulator-pin-moves](2026-10-23-when-an-emulator-pin-moves.md): the black patches were seen on the TV. The fix is Canary's, merged into Edge on 2026-10-09, and off by default. One of its two settings differs by game: the Unreal games need the window offset off, while Halo 4, Toy Story 3 and Aliens: Colonial Marines need it on, for a bottom-strip depth fix Edge added on 2026-10-01. Off is how every game drew at the earlier pin, so no game draws worse than it did here; those three keep the fault they always had. No game on Canary's compatibility list is reported worse with sample positions on. Weighed and not taken: Edge's default, which bands the bottom of the Unreal games; a settings file per game, against one rule for every game; the accuracy render path (fragment shader interlock), which the A9's Radeon has and which may fix both groups and Mass Effect 2 and Legendary as well, but changes how every game draws, is slower by design and was never measured. System Link, on by default since the earlier pin, needs no setting: Edge starts with no network ([emulators-get-no-network](2026-10-26-emulators-get-no-network.md)).
- **Numbers:** #632, "Xbox 360: Gears of War and other Unreal Engine 3 games without black patches"; xenia-canary/xenia-canary#1271, "[GPU] Xenos sample positions; UE3 tiling fix"; xenia-canary/game-compatibility#44 and #46, Gears of War and Gears of War 2; docs/PROJECT.md question 34.
- **Replaces:** the plan in docs/PROJECT.md question 34, "Found on the way", that the next Edge pin carrying Canary's change would pass the two settings for every game.
