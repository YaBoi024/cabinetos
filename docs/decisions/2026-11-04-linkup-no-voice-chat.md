# LinkUp has no voice chat

- **Decided:** No voice chat, ever. Friends talk over a phone or Discord.
- **Date:** 2026-11-04
- **Why:** People already have both, and they work. A microphone path on a family console is more to secure and maintain for nothing a phone does not already do.
- **Numbers:** #654, "LinkUp: play a game with a friend on another CabinetOS console over the internet".
- **Replaces:** none.
