# After 10 quiet minutes a LinkUp game pauses, and 15 more end the session

- **Decided:** After 10 minutes with no press from either player, the game pauses on both screens; one press and Resume carry on. If nobody presses anything for 15 more minutes, LinkUp ends the session and frees the relay, and the host's game stays paused on the TV, so nothing is lost. During a game a press on either console counts on both, so neither screen dims or turns off, and the idle rule is the only clock.
- **Date:** 2026-11-02
- **Why:** 10 minutes without a press on either console is far longer than play, and through the relay those minutes are a full-quality stream nobody watches, up to about 1.3 GB per 10 minutes on the owner's upload. The second wait is the console's default "Turn off screen after". A host who is watching must not have the picture dim while the friend plays, and the friend's presses never pass through the host's own controllers.
- **Numbers:** #654, "LinkUp: play a game with a friend on another CabinetOS console over the internet"; PR #666, "LinkUp: the console's side, step 3 of #654".
- **Replaces:** none.
