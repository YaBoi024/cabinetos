# The LinkUp friend is always player two, and the game stays the host's

- **Decided:** Two players only: the host's player one, and the friend as player two on every system. The seat exists from launch whenever inviting. Any other controller on the host's console does nothing and says nothing from the moment the friend joins until the session ends, though it still moves the menus. There is no third seat, whatever the game allows. Saves, save states and achievements belong to the host: the friend is a controller, nothing from the game is kept on their console or sent to their RomM account, and rewind, fast forward and loading a state stay allowed.
- **Date:** 2026-11-04
- **Why:** Many systems have only two ports, so a third seat would work on some games and not others, and one rule covers every game. Emulators run as programs get their controllers once at launch, and many games read players at the title, so a late player two is not a normal case. The game runs only on the host's console, under the host's account, so the friend's own saves are never at risk.
