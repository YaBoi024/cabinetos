# A LinkUp game starts by inviting an Online friend, and the console chases nobody

- **Decided:** Pressing an Online friend, then a game, is the one way to invite: there is no Host button and no open room, and the word host stays off the screen. Only that friend can join, and only Online friends can be invited. The game starts when the friend joins, so the inviter sits on a waiting screen for 30 minutes with B to cancel and no Start now, and the friend may decline. The console chases nobody: no Nudge, no alert when a friend comes online, no reminders and no invites kept for later.
- **Date:** 2026-11-04
- **Why:** LinkUp works like going to a friend's house: you knock, maybe they can't play today, and you go home. It is for friends you actually know, who talk to each other and have usually arranged it already, by phone, text or Discord, so the arranging happens outside the console and everything after it takes almost no effort. The words on screen stay plain, and the story goes in the wiki page. A game has one seat for a friend, so letting any friend join would let whoever pressed first take it, and no room exists until someone invites, so leaving LinkUp on lets nobody in. Both players begin at the title screen, because many games read how many are playing there and an emulator run as its own program gets its controllers only once.
- **Numbers:** #654, "LinkUp: play a game with a friend on another CabinetOS console over the internet"; PR #666, "LinkUp: the console's side, step 3 of #654".
- **Replaces:** the earlier flow, in which the host pressed Host, picked a game and then a friend, the game started at once, and an invite to an Offline friend waited until they were back.
