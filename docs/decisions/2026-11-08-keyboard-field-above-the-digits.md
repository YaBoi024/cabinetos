# Up from the digits is the field, on every keyboard

- **Decided:** On every on-screen keyboard, Search's included, Up from the digits moves focus into the field, where Left and Right move a cursor and Down or A goes back to the key focus left. Typing, the shortcut keys and del act at the cursor. On Search, Up from the field goes on to the results.
- **Date:** 2026-11-08
- **Why:** The field had no cursor, so a typo early in a long address or password was fixed only by deleting back to it and typing the rest again. Up from the digits went nowhere, so it was free on every keyboard but Search's, where it went to the results. The options for Search were keeping Up from the digits as the way to the results and reaching its field some other way, or the one rule everywhere at the cost of one more press on the way up. One rule was picked: a keyboard that behaves one way in Search and another everywhere else has to be learned twice, and the extra press is on a route a player takes once per search. The field shows focus with the focus rim inside its edge, and the cursor is drawn over the text, so nothing moves or changes size when focus goes into the field or the cursor walks through it.
- **Numbers:** #668, "On-screen keyboard: a held direction does not repeat, and Shift cannot stay on" (the cursor is in its comment of 2026-11-08).
- **Replaces:** Up from the digits going to Search's results (`App::keyboardUp`, removed), and nowhere on other keyboards.
