# LinkUp's picture is H.265 at the host's own size, up to 1080p

- **Decided:** The picture is H.265 at the host's own resolution, capped at 1080p, with H.264 as the fallback. A 4K host is shrunk to 1080p by asking gamescope for that size, with no 4K copy; a 1080p or 720p host is streamed as it is. How far the picture may fall when the connection is poor is in [linkup-picture-floor](2026-11-07-linkup-picture-floor.md).
- **Date:** 2026-11-02
- **Why:** H.265 needs about a third less data than H.264 for the same picture, and 1080p is what the bitrates were measured at. H.264 stays for a friend's console that cannot decode H.265.
- **Numbers:** #654, "LinkUp: play a game with a friend on another CabinetOS console over the internet"; PR #666, "LinkUp: the console's side, step 3 of #654".
- **Replaces:** none.
