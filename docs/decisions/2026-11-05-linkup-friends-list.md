# LinkUp's list orders friends by status and gives each a colour from their name

- **Decided:** The list shows Invited, Online, Linked up and Offline, in that order, alphabetical within each, and focus follows the person. Linked up starts when you invite and ends if nobody joins. A game played alone is not shown. Each friend has a colour worked out from their name, so nothing is stored or sent, on solid tiles at one brightness to the eye; eight colours stay clearly apart before one repeats. Offline is a plain grey, and an invite has a steady amber edge. With no friends the screen stays, with a picture, "Player two, from anywhere." and "Whoever runs your server adds friends", and a first friend arriving fades it away.
- **Date:** 2026-11-05
- **Why:** People see who is available without seeing what anyone plays alone. A name in a colour is found at a glance from a sofa, and a solid tile keeps the background from muddying it. The empty screen says LinkUp works and that someone else adds friends, and a person there has nothing to press.
- **Numbers:** #654, "LinkUp: play a game with a friend on another CabinetOS console over the internet"; PR #666, "LinkUp: the console's side, step 3 of #654".
- **Replaces:** the first plan's friend shown by name alone, with no colour, and the status In a LinkUp game.
