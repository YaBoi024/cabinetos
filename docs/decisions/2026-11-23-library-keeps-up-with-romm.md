# Home and the Library ask RomM again while the console is on

- **Decided:** Home and the Library ask RomM again as they come into view, as somebody picks up the controller after a minute with no press, or as RomM answers after being away: one read at a time, none sooner than a minute after the last ended, and nothing on a timer, in a game or offline. A read is five small requests, and its answer is put into the library in place, part by part; a part RomM did not answer for stays as it was. The highlight stays on the game or tile it was on; on Resume it stays on the first cover, and A does nothing for a second after another game takes that place.
- **Date:** 2026-11-23
- **Why:** on one console, what its owner changed in RomM, and what was played in RomM's web player or another app, showed only after a restart. A timer, or RomM asked in the background, would ask servers about screens nobody is looking at, and people's servers are small. RomM has no list of what was deleted, so its short lists are read whole, and what costs is left out: every favourite's entry (23 KB each; the person's Favorites collection says which games they are), a system's list, a cover for a tile that has one, and the autogenerated collections. Measured on the reference server: 0.23 s and 96 KB compressed. Swapping the whole library in, as coming back online does, was the first plan: it emptied a row whose request failed, forgot the lists already read, refused an account switch while it ran, and a press in that frame could open another game. In place, the cards are only ever added to, so every index a list, Search or a game's page holds stays its game. The highlight does not follow the game off Resume, because the first cover is what Home opens on and is one press from playing; the second is for a press already on its way to the game that was there.
- **Numbers:** #730, "Home and the Library: what changes in RomM shows only after a restart".
- **Replaces:** none.
