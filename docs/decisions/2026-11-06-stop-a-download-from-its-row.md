# A download stops from its own row, after a question

- **Decided:** On a game's page, pressing A on the row that is filling asks "Stop downloading <game>?" with Keep going focused and Stop beside it; Stop ends the download, as Escape on a keyboard already did. B stays Back, and no other button stops a download.
- **Date:** 2026-11-06
- **Why:** A download could be stopped only by Escape on a keyboard or by ejecting its drive. On a pad the filling row did nothing, B walked away with the download still running, and no other game could download until it ended, so a wrong press on a big game on slow Wi-Fi tied the console up for an hour (a player's request). The options: X stopping it in one press (free, since X does nothing on any screen, but a button nobody has learned on this console, the row would need words saying so, and a stray press throws away whatever had arrived); B stopping it (which breaks "walk away and it finishes quietly", 2026-09-21); or A on the row with a question. The question needs no hint, because the row you pressed is the row anyone presses again, and it guards the bytes the way Remove download's question does (#188). One wording for every stage, unpacking and installing included, because a player calls all of it the download.
- **Numbers:** PR #663, "A download can be stopped from its row"; a player's request, 2026-11-06.
- **Replaces:** the filling row doing nothing (`detailscreen.cpp`, since #185).
