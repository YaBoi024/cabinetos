# A download stops from its own row, after a question

- **Decided:** On a game's page, pressing A on the row that is filling asks "Stop this download?" with Cancel and Stop, Cancel focused and nothing under the question; Stop ends the download, as Escape on a keyboard already did. B stays Back, and no other button stops a download.
- **Date:** 2026-11-06
- **Why:** A download could be stopped only by Escape on a keyboard or by ejecting its drive. On a pad the filling row did nothing, B walked away with the download still running, and no other game could download until it ended, so a wrong press on a big game on slow Wi-Fi tied the console up for an hour (a player's request). The options: X stopping it in one press (free, since X does nothing on any screen, but a button nobody has learned on this console, the row would need words saying so, and a stray press throws away whatever had arrived); B stopping it (which breaks "walk away and it finishes quietly", 2026-09-21); or A on the row with a question. The question needs no hint, because the row you pressed is the row anyone presses again, and it guards the bytes the way Remove download's question does (#188). One wording for every stage, unpacking and installing included, because a player calls all of it the download. The words are the console's usual ones, judged on the TV the same day: a verb and the thing, "this" for a thing already on the screen (as "Format this drive?" and "Remove this Wii Remote?"), Cancel first as the storage boxes have it, and no line under the question; the first version named the game and showed its size, which made the box wide for nothing under the game's own title, and said "Keep going", which no other box says.
- **Numbers:** PR #663, "A download can be stopped from its row"; a player's request, 2026-11-06.
- **Replaces:** the filling row doing nothing (`detailscreen.cpp`, since #185).
